1. Overview

Big Timer is a free, high-visibility Progressive Web App countdown timer built for public speakers, presenters, and performers. Unlike a standard phone timer, Big Timer is designed to be read from a distance and uses visual alerts (screen flashing) instead of sound, so you can keep track of time without checking small numbers or listening for beeps.

The app runs entirely in your browser. There is no sign-in, no account, and no tracking. PWA Offline ready

2. Getting Started

Set a duration

Type your desired time in MM:SS format (for example, 05:00 for 5 minutes) into the duration field, or tap one of the quick-set buttons: 1m, 2m, 3m, 5m, 10m, 20m, 30m.

Start the timer

  1. Enter or choose your duration.
  2. Select a theme (Dark, Light, or Custom).
  3. Press the START button. The countdown will fill the screen.

You can also start a timer directly from a link or bookmark by adding a ?duration= parameter, for example /?duration=05:00 for a 5-minute countdown.

3. Themes and Colors

Choose the look that works best for your venue and lighting.

  • Dark: Black background with white text (default). Best for dark stages.
  • Light: White background with black text. Best for bright rooms.
  • Custom: Pick your own background and text colors using the color pickers.

High-contrast presets ensure readability in almost any lighting condition, from a dark stage to a bright conference room.

4. Focus Mode

When the timer starts, the interface strips away everything except the countdown. The digits are shown in the largest possible font (using the Roboto Condensed font) to fill the viewport, taking at least 90% of the screen.

  • Exit button: Tap the small × to return to the configuration screen.
  • Fullscreen button: Tap the ⛶ button for maximum visibility.

5. Visual Alerts

To keep you on track without audio, Big Timer flashes the screen at key moments:

  • 2 minutes left: Screen flashes 4 times (200ms cycles).
  • 1 minute left: Screen flashes 4 times (200ms cycles).
  • 30 seconds left: Continuous flashing until the clock reaches zero.
  • Overtime: After 00:00, the timer counts upward. The background turns solid red and the text turns solid white to signal urgency.

6. Fullscreen Mode

While the timer is running, press the ⛶ button to toggle fullscreen for a borderless, immersive view. Press it again, or press Esc, to exit fullscreen.

7. Install and Offline Use

Big Timer is a Progressive Web App, which means it can be installed to your device and used without an internet connection once loaded.

How to install

  • iOS: Open in Safari, tap the Share button, then choose "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android / Chrome: Open the browser menu and choose "Install App".
  • Desktop: Installable as a desktop app via Chrome, Edge, or other supported browsers.

After installation, Big Timer runs full screen and works offline.

8. App Shortcuts

Once Big Timer is installed, quick-start shortcuts are available from the app icon on supported devices. These open the app with a preset duration already loaded:

  • 5 min - starts a 5-minute countdown.
  • 10 min - starts a 10-minute countdown.
  • 30 min - starts a 30-minute countdown.

On desktop browsers, long-press or right-click the app icon to see these shortcuts.

9. Preferences

Big Timer remembers your last used settings automatically, including your timer duration, selected theme, and custom colors. These are stored locally in your browser using localStorage, so they remain available the next time you open the app.

To reset your preferences, clear your browser's site data for Big Timer. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is stored.

10. Troubleshooting

The timer is not full screen

Press the ⛶ button during focus mode. If that does not help, make sure your browser supports fullscreen and that another tab or window is not already in fullscreen.

The digits look wrong

Big Timer uses the Roboto Condensed font. If it has not loaded yet (for example, on a slow or offline first visit), the browser will show a fallback font. Once the font is cached, it will always load correctly, even offline.

The app does not work offline

Make sure the page has loaded at least once while online so the service worker can cache the app files. If problems persist, clear the site data and visit the app online again.

Screen does not flash

Visual alerts use color inversion. Some accessibility settings or browser extensions can affect this. Try switching themes or disabling browser extensions that override page colors.
